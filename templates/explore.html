Our Application vs. Market Alternatives

DermaDetect leverages state-of-the-art Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), similar to commercial apps like SkinVision. However, we have built this platform as an accessible, open investigation tool for educational purposes.

While SkinVision requires a subscription and is CE-marked in Europe, our tool allows you to freely investigate and understand the deep learning capabilities of skin lesion classification.

How the AI Works

Our model is trained on the HAM10000 dataset, containing thousands of dermatoscopic images across 7 different diagnostic categories. When you upload an image, the image is resized, normalized, and passed through our trained neural network. Additionally, demographic data like Age and Biological Sex are encoded and used to increase the model's confidence.
